# Disclaimer

Thunai Care is an early prototype for information and discussion only.

- It is **not medical advice** and is not a substitute for a qualified doctor.
- It **does not judge** whether any doctor's treatment is right or wrong. Many flagged patterns are sometimes appropriate care.
- **Never stop, skip or change a medicine on your own.** Some psychiatric medicines, including benzodiazepines, antidepressants and antipsychotics, can cause serious withdrawal effects if stopped suddenly. Any change must be planned with a doctor.
- The red-flag rules have **not yet been reviewed by a practising psychiatrist** for use in this tool.

If you or someone you know is in crisis in India, call **Tele-MANAS on 14416** (free, 24×7, multiple languages) or go to the nearest emergency department.
