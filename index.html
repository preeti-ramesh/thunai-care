Welcome. It's okay to have questions about care.

Thunai helps you see how prescriptions have changed over time, so you can ask clear questions. Before you start, three promises:

  • Private. Everything stays on this device. Nothing is uploaded — even photos are read here.
  • No names needed. Use a nickname. Doctors are just "A" or "B".
  • No blame. This doesn't judge any doctor, and it's not medical advice. Never stop a medicine on your own.

1. Add visits

Add one visit at a time. You can always come back and change anything.

Family or friend observations optional

People close to the patient often notice changes first. Describe what you saw — please avoid names of people or places.

2. What the pattern shows

Thunai Care is an early open prototype. Pattern checks are based on published guidance (American Psychiatric Association Choosing Wisely, Alabama Psychiatric Medication Review Team, NHS England STOMP, UK guidance on sedatives) plus a few project heuristics awaiting clinical review. See docs/red-flag-criteria.md and docs/trust-and-safety.md.

Private by design: your data stays in this browser. Reading a photo downloads an open-source reader (Tesseract) the first time; the photo itself never leaves your device.
